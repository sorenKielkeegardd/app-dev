# Fight Club (1999)

## Overview

**Fight Club** is a *psychological thriller* directed by **David Fincher**, based on the 1996 novel by Chuck Palahniuk. It follows an unnamed narrator who is stuck in a numbing corporate life until he meets the charismatic Tyler Durden.

***It's a film about identity, consumerism, and masculinity.***
